## Golden rules for negotiating property with complete peace of mind

Most owners and agents in Venezuela work professionally and in good faith, but online it always pays to apply strict checks before sharing sensitive documents or transferring any sum of money.

### 5 red flags that mean stop the transaction

1. **Unrealistically low prices with pressure to act fast**: offers 40 % or 50 % below the area, paired with lines like _"I have three people interested today, transfer the money to hold it without viewing it"_.
2. **Requests for money upfront to view the property**: never pay to "book a viewing", for "key handling" or to "hold it without an in-person visit".
3. **Refusing to show original ownership documents**: before any formal reservation, a lawyer you trust should review the registered title and the identity of the signatory.
4. **Third-party bank accounts with no link to the property**: be suspicious if the person receiving funds does not appear on the title deed or hold a valid registered power of attorney.
5. **Viewings at night or in isolated spots**: always book viewings in broad daylight, take someone with you and tell a relative or business partner where you are.
