## Where active property demand is concentrated

Residential and corporate demand in Venezuela shows a clear preference for established neighbourhoods with good road links, shops nearby and autonomy over basic services — constant water, power backup and fibre internet.

### Greater Caracas: executive, diplomatic and family profiles

In [Caracas](/caracas) and the eastern metropolitan corridor, three distinct search profiles stand out:

- **Modern executive apartments**: high rental and sales turnover in financial and restaurant districts, with newer buildings, strict security and backup generators.
- **Family houses and townhouses**: quiet neighbourhoods with a mountain climate, large gardens and schools close by.
- **Street-level offices and retail units**: ready-to-operate spaces with visitor parking on established commercial corridors.

### Carabobo: Valencia and San Diego as residential and industrial hubs

The Carabobo market combines business dynamism, universities and gated communities with a high quality of life:

- **[Valencia](/valencia), northern district**: neighbourhoods such as El Parral, La Trigaleña, El Bosque, Prebo, Guataparo and La Viña account for much of the demand for apartments with backup generator, water well, and shopping centres and clinics nearby.
- **[San Diego](/san-diego)**: one of the most requested municipalities for **townhouses, houses in gated communities, plots of land and industrial warehouses**, valued for its road planning, cool climate and proximity to universities and industrial zones.
