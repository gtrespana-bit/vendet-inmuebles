## How to structure a transparent rental in Venezuela

The **residential and commercial rental market in Venezuela** draws a clear line between properties used as a home and those used as retail space, offices or warehouses. Knowing which framework applies to each case prevents misunderstandings and protects both landlord and tenant.

### Housing vs. commercial use: the key differences

| Aspect | Residential lease | Commercial unit / Office |
|---|---|---|
| Main legal framework | Housing lease regulation and control law | Commercial lease regulation decree / Civil Code |
| Usual term | Minimum 1 year, renewable under the applicable law | Term agreed by the parties (usually 1 to 3 years) |
| Condominium fees | The contract states who pays services and routine maintenance | Share of common expenses and operating costs detailed in the contract |
| Attached inventory | Essential, with photographs of finishes, appliances and air conditioning | Condition of electrical installations, backup generator, HVAC and façade |

### Checklist before signing the contract

- **Verifiable identity and references**: request national ID, tax ID, checkable employment or trade references and, for companies, the incorporation deed and latest valid shareholders' resolution.
- **Handover report with photographs**: document the condition of paintwork, locks, taps, air conditioning units, water heater and meter readings on the day the keys are handed over.
- **Permitted use of the property**: put in writing whether it is strictly residential or commercial, the building's house rules, assigned parking spaces and the policy on pets or refurbishment.
- **Formal payment channels and receipts**: agree in writing which accounts will receive the rent and issue a receipt for every monthly payment.
