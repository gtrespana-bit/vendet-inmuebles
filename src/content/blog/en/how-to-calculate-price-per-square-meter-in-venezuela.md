## Why the asking price defines how fast you sell

One of the most common mistakes when listing a property is setting the price based on what it cost ten years ago, or on expectations unsupported by recent closings. Buyers compare dozens of listings within minutes, and a property priced more than **15 % above its micro-area** loses traction during the critical first weeks on the market.

### The variables that move the price per square metre in Venezuela today

1. **Continuity of essential utilities**: buildings or gated communities with a **deep water well**, a **backup generator** (for common areas or the whole building) and fibre optic internet command a significantly higher price and turnover.
2. **Security and access control**: gated streets with 24/7 guards, CCTV and a solvent condominium board sustain a higher price per square metre.
3. **Condition of kitchen, bathrooms and installations**: a move-in-ready property with updated plumbing and wiring saves the buyer months of building work and budget uncertainty.
4. **Covered parking spaces and storage**: in dense areas of Caracas, Valencia or Lechería, a second covered space can make the difference against similar properties.

### How to run your own comparative market analysis in 4 steps

| Step | What to compare | Suggested adjustment |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Pick 5 to 8 comparables | Same neighbourhood or within a 1 km radius, same property type | Discard listings that are clearly out of market |
| 2. Work out the asking USD/m² | Divide the listed price by the real built area | Separate uncovered terraces or extra land |
| 3. Adjust for utilities and floor | Well, generator, views, low/high floor and age | Add or subtract between 5 % and 15 % depending on features |
| 4. Negotiation margin | Gap between asking price and actual closing | Keep a realistic margin of 5 % to 8 % |
