## The first 5 photos decide whether a buyer opens your listing

On a property portal, the cover photo and the headline compete directly for the buyer's attention. A well-documented listing does not just get more clicks: it filters out time-wasters and attracts viewings from genuinely qualified buyers.

### Practical rules for photographing your property with a phone or camera

- **Landscape format and daylight**: open curtains and blinds, switch on warm supporting lights and shoot from corners at chest height to show the real sense of space without distortion.
- **Tidy rooms and clear surfaces**: remove personal items, visible cables, cleaning products in bathrooms and cars parked in front of the façade before you start shooting.
- **A logical gallery order**:
  1. Façade, main living room or the terrace with the best light (cover photo).
  2. Full kitchen and integrated living area.
  3. Main bedroom and main bathroom.
  4. Secondary bedrooms, parking spaces and communal areas (pool, lobby, garden, water well or generator).

### What must never be missing from the description and fact sheet

| Key data | Why it increases enquiries |
|---|---|
| Real built and total area | Lets buyers filter and compare value per m² transparently |
| Bedrooms, bathrooms and parking spaces | Answers immediately whether the property suits the family |
| Building or community services | Stating **water well**, **backup generator**, mains gas or security puts your listing into the most-used filters |
| Approximate location on the map | Builds instant trust without exposing the exact house or apartment number |
